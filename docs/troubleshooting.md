# Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Download a chart region first (Regions)." | Tap **Regions** and download the region for your trip. |
| A banner says the trip needs another region | Tap its **Download** button, or download it from the Regions screen. |
| "Start must be LAT, LON" | Type decimal degrees with a comma (`39.25, -76.48`) or hemispheres (`39.25 N, 76.48 W`), or use **Places** / **Here**. |
| "No location fix" | Turn on Location, wait for a GPS fix outdoors, or type the position. |
| "No route found ..." | Read the explanation: it names what boxes the point in (shallow water, a hazard buffer, land, a low bridge). Move the point into open water, lower the safe depth if the boat allows, or check the air draft. |
| "... is in open ocean, and Stay inside is on" | The point is outside the lines across bay mouths and inlets (outside the Golden Gate, off a beach). Turn off **Stay inside**, or move the point inside. |
| "No navigable water within 3000 m of ... for this depth" | Nothing within 3 km is deep enough for your safe depth. Lower the depth if the boat allows, or move the point. If the message also mentions Stay inside, open ocean nearby is ruled out too. |
| A point moved away from where you put it | It was on land or a dock, or in a pocket of water cut off from the rest. The report says how far it moved. |
| The route goes a way you would not | Check the report notes and the chart. If it looks wrong, [report it](report-a-problem.html). |
| OsmAnd does not open the route | Tap **Show routes folder in Files** and open the `.gpx` from there, choosing OsmAnd. |
| A chart map imported but nothing shows in OsmAnd | Turn on the Online maps plugin, select the chart as overlay, and move the transparency slider off the far left. |
| Changes do not appear after a chart map update | Delete the old copy in OsmAnd (Maps & Resources > Local) and open the new file. |
| All lights look magenta on the paper-chart map | Normal paper-chart symbols. Read the color from the label. |
| "Out of memory" | Plan shorter pieces, or add via points. |
| "Not enough space" for a chart map | Free up space; converting needs about 2.8 times the map's size. |
