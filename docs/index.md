# NOAA Route user guide

**NOAA Route** plans boat routes on your Android phone from NOAA's official vector charts (ENC). You give it a start, a destination, your safe operating depth and your air draft. It plans a route that stays in deep enough water, keeps off shoals, rocks and wrecks, passes channel markers on the correct side and only goes under bridges you clear. Then it hands the route to OsmAnd+ as a GPX file.

Once your chart region is downloaded, planning works fully offline, usually in a second or two.

{: .warning }
> **Not a certified navigation system.** NOAA Route is a planning aid. Depths are charted at low water (MLLW) with no tide added, soundings are sparse, and the bottom changes. Check every leg against a current chart before you run it, and keep your eyes on the water. See [Safety and limits](safety.html).
